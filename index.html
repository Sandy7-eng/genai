COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything You Need To Know

How LeftClick protects your domain, ensures compliance, and scales your outbound pipeline.

Paid advertising requires continuous auction bidding where Google and LinkedIn capture all the margin. Hiring in-house SDRs requires $100k-$120k OTE per rep plus $15k/yr in tech stack licenses and months of ramp time. LeftClick provides a completely managed, software-driven outbound engine that reaches thousands of verified decision makers with zero ad spend and zero payroll overhead — delivering qualified meetings at a fraction of traditional CAC ($95 - $190 vs $700+).

Instead of shotgunning cold emails to every name on a list, our "Pick Best Fit" algorithm filters your total addressable market through multiple intent checkpoints: funding timing, hiring signals, active tech stack integrations, and job changes. We only initiate outreach with accounts that have an active, urgent business reason to evaluate your solution today.

Generic cold email templates or basic “Hey {{first_name}} at {{company}}” tags get ignored or flagged as spam. Our AI pipeline reads recent 10-K filings, press releases, LinkedIn posts, job descriptions, and podcasts to extract authentic context. It synthesizes an original, contextually relevant opening hook for every single prospect that is indistinguishable from a top-tier AE doing 25 minutes of manual research.

Never. We never send outbound cold emails from your primary corporate domain. We purchase, configure, authenticate, and warm up 20 to 50 lookalike secondary domains (e.g., `getcompany.com`, `trycompany.co`) with complete SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and custom tracking domains. Your primary corporate email stays 100% clean and isolated.

Domain setup and automated warming take approximately 10-14 days to reach peak deliverability scores. Once campaign sequencing goes live, high-growth B2B clients typically see their first qualified meetings appearing on their calendar within the first 48 to 72 hours of launch.

Before any prospect is booked onto your calendar, they must pass your predefined ICP qualification criteria (title, company size, revenue, tech stack). If a meeting ever slips through that doesn't meet the agreed specifications or is a no-show, we replace it at zero cost.
